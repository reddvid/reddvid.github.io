7. Your Rights Under the Data Privacy Act

As a data subject under Philippine law, you have the following rights:

7.1 Right to Information

  • Be informed about the collection and processing of your personal data
  • Know the purpose, extent, and method of data processing

7.2 Right to Access

  • Request a copy of your personal data we hold
  • Obtain information about how your data is being processed

7.3 Right to Correction

  • Update or correct inaccurate personal information
  • Complete incomplete data records

7.4 Right to Erasure or Blocking

  • Request deletion of your personal data under certain circumstances
  • Block processing of your data when appropriate

7.5 Right to Object

  • Object to the processing of your personal data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing (where consent is the legal basis)

7.6 Right to Data Portability

  • Receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used format
  • Transfer your data to another controller when technically feasible
Exercising Your Rights: To exercise any of these rights, contact our Privacy Team at privacy@relive3d.com. We will respond to your request within fifteen (15) days as required by the DPA.
