Step into the Activation Area, where imagination meets motion. With Clickeys, guests can design their own 3D keychains or keycaps, blending fidget toy elements, vibrant charms, and custom 3D-printed designs to create fun and functional collectibles that reflect their unique personalities.

From DIY enthusiasts to creative guests looking for something new, Clickeys transforms every event into an unforgettable, hands-on experience — one where creativity clicks into place.

**2. We Go to Your Event**

## **What Happens During Your Event**

**1. Visit the Clickeys Booth**

Guests drop by the Clickeys station — a creative corner waiting just for them!

**2. Choose Your Base**

They pick their keychain or keycap foundation — from fun fidget styles to sleek customizable pieces.

**3. Customize & Mix**

Guests select their favorite colors, charms, or mini 3D icons to match their mood or the event’s theme.

**4. Assemble & Click Together**

They build their own unique Clickey — twist, snap, and play with their personalized creation!

**5. Take It Home!**

Each guest takes home their Clickey as a memento of the event — a small token of creativity, ready to fidget and flaunt!
