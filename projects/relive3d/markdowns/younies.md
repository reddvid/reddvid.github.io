## How Younies Works (Step-by-Step)

**1. Book Your Session**

Choose between an event activation (weddings, awards nights, reunions) or a private session for your family and friends. We’ll coordinate the schedule, location, and setup details for your shoot.

**2. We Come to You**

Our team arrives at your venue and sets up the Re:Live 3D Pop-Up Studio — professional, compact, and perfect for capturing memories on-site.

**3. Get Scanned in 3D**

Step in for a quick and contactless full-body scan! Our system captures every angle in high-resolution color to perfectly recreate your look and pose.

**4. Preview & Personalize**

After scanning, you’ll preview your digital model, choose your preferred size and quantity, and finalize your order through our secure checkout process.

**5. We Craft Your Younies**

Our skilled artists and engineers refine, process, and 3D print your figurines using advanced full-color technology — ensuring every detail is spot-on.

**6. Receive in 1 Week**

Your lifelike Younies are ready within 1 week after your event or session. You can have them delivered via courier or pick them up directly.

**7. Unbox, Review & Tag Us!**

Once you receive your Younie, share your excitement online — post your photos, tag @ReLive3D, and let others see your moment brought to life!
